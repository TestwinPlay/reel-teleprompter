Reel Teleprompter

Keys: Space play/pause text · ↑/↓ one line · ←/→ or PageUp/PageDown two lines (presenter clicker) · R record/stop · P pause/resume recording. Trackpad scroll over the text also moves it. Prompter text, safe zone and highlight are not recorded. Output is MP4 when the browser supports it, otherwise WebM. Recording happens on your device; video and scripts are never uploaded.

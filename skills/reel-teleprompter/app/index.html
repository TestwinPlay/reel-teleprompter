Record Instagram Reels from your laptop, with a teleprompter.

Read your script while looking at the camera. Get a ready-to-post 1080×1920 video, with no cropping in an editor afterwards. Free, no sign-up, no watermark.

Open the teleprompter

Works in Chrome and Safari on Mac and Windows laptops.

Why use it

Exact Reel size9:16 (1080×1920), 4:5 feed, 1:1 or 16:9. Your landscape webcam is cropped for you.
Studio-clean audioPick your USB or wireless lapel mic. Built-in AI noise removal takes out fan, AC and hiss without the muffled call-style sound.
Instagram safe zoneSee where the caption and buttons will cover your video before you record.
Never lose your placeScroll back with the trackpad, arrow keys, a presenter clicker or your phone as a remote.
Countdown, pause, review3-2-1 start, pause and resume into one file, and watch the take before you download.
Screen + you, split screenRecord any window or your whole screen with your face below it, beside it, or in a bubble. Circle, highlight and draw arrows while you record.
Your reel, PDF or photo behind youAdd videos, images and PDFs as a slideshow beside you, or cut yourself out and stand in front of them. No green screen needed.
Private by designRecording happens in your browser. Video and scripts never leave your device.

How it works

  1. Open the teleprompter and allow camera and microphone.
  2. Paste your script and pick 9:16 Reels.
  3. Press Record. The text scrolls under your camera so your eyes stay on the lens.
  4. Stop, watch the take, download the MP4 and post it.
